PROJECT 01

Medicine Booking System

A user-friendly medicine booking system designed to make medicine ordering easier for patients, elderly people and users who may need assistance.

  • Users can choose their preferred pharmacy.
  • Users can check medicine availability.
  • Users can submit their medicine requirements.
  • Pharmacist assistance is provided to help patients.

Purpose

The project aims to make medicine ordering easier and more accessible for patients, elderly people and users who require additional support.

Pharmacy Selection

Users can select the pharmacy they prefer instead of being restricted to a single pharmacy.

Medicine Availability

Users can check whether the required medicine is available at the selected pharmacy.

Patient Assistance

The system is designed with simple interaction so patients can easily communicate their medicine requirements.

Pharmacist Guidance

The pharmacist can assist the patient by providing information about the medicines and explaining how they should be taken.

Voice Assistance

A voice assistance concept can make the system easier to use for people who find typing difficult.

Main Benefit

The system provides a convenient connection between patients and pharmacies while making medicine booking simpler.

PROJECT 02

Scholarship Recommendation System

A recommendation system that helps students discover suitable scholarships based on their eligibility.

  • Students can enter their details.
  • Scholarships can be matched with eligibility.
  • Suitable scholarships can be prioritized.
  • Important scholarship information can be organized.

Purpose

The project helps students find suitable scholarship opportunities without having to search through many different sources.

Eligibility Checking

Student information can be used to identify scholarships for which the student may be eligible.

Personalized Recommendation

Suitable scholarships are recommended according to the student's eligibility and provided details.

Priority Listing

Scholarships can be displayed according to eligibility and priority so students can focus on the most relevant opportunities first.

Easy Scholarship Search

The system reduces the time and effort required to search for suitable scholarships.

Application Information

Important scholarship details and application requirements can be presented clearly in one place.

Main Benefit

The system helps students discover relevant scholarship opportunities more efficiently and makes the search process easier.
